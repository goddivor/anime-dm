ADM checks for a new version on its own, a few seconds after each start. When it finds one, it shows what it brings and offers to install it. You can also look for it at any time with Help › Quick update. The technical details of each version are in the changelog.

What's new in version 0.02

Released on October 4, 2026

  • The icon of an anime's folder follows it when it is renamed, moved, or copied to another disk. Folders dressed by version 0.01 are repaired at startup.
  • The Categories panel remembers folded animes from one session to the next.
  • Deleting an anime with its files also removes its folder, if it holds nothing but its icon and the Aniyomi files. A folder where you put anything else is left untouched.
  • ADM now starts with Windows, in the notification area. The box in Options › General turns it off.

What's new in version 0.01

Released on September 25, 2026

The first version of the Windows application, written in C++ to replace the former cross-platform application.

  • Download engine with dynamic segmentation: eight connections per video, three videos at once, resume after a cut, HLS playlists without ffmpeg.
  • Sources installed from the Addon Store and verified by SHA-256 checksum: Voir-Anime, French-Stream, and Wiflix.
  • Extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Chromium, Vivaldi, Opera, and Firefox: a button on anime and episode pages, and a panel when hovering links.
  • Adding in three independent windows, and batch download from the clipboard.
  • Categories panel with the posters, a list with customizable columns, a choice of font, dark and light themes.
  • Scheduler with two queues, and following of animes that are still airing.
  • Folder icons made from the posters, and preparation for Aniyomi.
  • Export and import as an ADM file, text, JSON, CSV, Excel, and OpenDocument.
  • Animated Classique Rem and Néon toolbars, and support for Internet Download Manager skins.
  • Notification area, download-complete notifications, installer, and built-in updates.
