Faster and more reliable

Install Anime Download Manager and stop waiting for an episode to finish. Each video is pulled through several connections at once, three episodes move forward together, and the next ones wait their turn in the queue. A network cut, a shutdown, or a power failure loses nothing: the download resumes where it stopped.

{{img main-dark "Main window of Anime Download Manager: three episodes of Sousou no Frieren downloading, the next ones pending"}}

A download engine with dynamic segmentation

ADM works like the major download managers, with plain HTTP requests and no ffmpeg. The file starts as a single segment; each connection that frees up splits the largest remaining segment in half, so none stays idle. Positions are saved every three seconds, and HLS playlists (.m3u8) follow the same path, AES-128 decryption included.

Right from your browser

The ADM extension for Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, and Mozilla Firefox adds a "Download with ADM" button to the page of an anime or an episode, and the same panel when you hover any link that leads there. One click, and the add window opens with the source chosen and the episode checked.

One episode, a season, or the whole series

Paste the address of an anime: ADM reads the page and shows the poster and the list of episodes. Check them in the grid or type a range such as 1-8,12. Right-click an episode to choose its player, and the Later button drops it into the queue without starting it.

Several addresses on the clipboard? Ctrl + Shift + V groups them by anime and opens an add window for each.

Your favorite anime sites

Each site is served by a source, a small library you install from the built-in Addon Store: Voir-Anime, French-Stream, Wiflix, and more to come. Every library carries a SHA-256 checksum, checked before it is even written to the disk, and the store only serves sources published by the project.

Scheduler and followed animes

Start a queue at the time you choose, once or every day, stop it at a set time, then let ADM quit or shut down the computer. Follow an anime that is still airing: on its release day ADM checks the page, then every three hours for two days, and adds each new episode on its own.

Each series in its own folder

The episodes of an anime land in its own folder, and the Categories panel shows them under its poster. ADM can also dress that folder with an icon made from the poster (DVD case, Blu-ray case, disc, folder) that follows it when you move it, and prepare each series for Aniyomi.
