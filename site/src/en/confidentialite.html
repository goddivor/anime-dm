Privacy policy

In short: Anime Download Manager collects nothing. It has no account, no usage statistics, no ads, and no tracker.

What stays on your computer

Your download list, your settings, your followed animes, the posters, and the installed sources are saved in %APPDATA%\anime-dm, on your computer, and nowhere else.

The only connections ADM makes

  • To the sites you use, and the hosts of their players, to read the pages and download the videos you asked for.
  • To GitHub, to read the Addon Store index, download a source, and check whether a new version of the application exists.

These requests carry no information about you beyond what any browser sends (IP address, software version).

The browser extension

The extension never talks to the network. It reads the address of the page on screen to know whether a source serves it, and only hands an address to the application installed on your computer when you click "Download with ADM."

This site

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookie and no audience measurement tool; it only stores your theme choice in your browser. GitHub may keep technical connection logs, under its own privacy policy.
